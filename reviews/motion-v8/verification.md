# Final motion checks

The last two pending checks were completed on 1 October 2026. Previously reviewed scenes, cats and other character actions were not rerun, following the request to finish the remaining work and publish.

Two independent reviewers examined the actual rendered frames, including the entire shoulder, elbow, wrist, clothing silhouette and ground contact. Both accepted the final versions below.

Interrupted withdrawal clears the book and returns to a complete standing pose. |

The first attempt still exposed a defect in the Night Research withdrawal. It was fixed with complete native pose drawings and then checked again from a fresh page load. The failed intermediate version is not the published result.

The evidence uses normal Enter, Places, theme, painted-avatar clicks and arrow keys in a fixed 1440 × 900 browser viewport. No action classes, pose seeking, slow-motion settings or fabricated intermediate frames were used. The videos preserve the timestamps of the real compositor screenshots with variable frame timing. The cloud browser uses software rendering; these captures verify the observed anatomy and transitions, and do not establish a hardware frame-rate benchmark.
